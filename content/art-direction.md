# The Long Run — Art Direction

## North star
An original 1980s-inspired action-cartoon / manga presentation: bold silhouettes, expressive faces, speed lines, dramatic poses, cel-shaded forms, limited punchy palette and hand-drawn energy. This is an original visual language, not a recreation of any existing franchise.

## Mara Vale
Mara must read instantly at thumbnail size. Red utility jacket, charcoal trousers, worn trainers, messenger bag and silver Courier 17 tag are non-negotiable anchors.

## Animation language
- 8–12 key poses per major action rather than hundreds of frames.
- Strong anticipation and exaggerated follow-through.
- 2–4 frame hit flashes for impact.
- Speed lines on jumps, dashes and vehicle transitions.
- Camera punch / screen shake reserved for meaningful moments.
- Dialogue uses short comic-panel bursts rather than long cutscenes.

## Environment language
Deep charcoal shadows, muted blue-grey city structures, warm street lights and the red jacket as the dominant accent. Backgrounds should feel hand-illustrated while gameplay remains performant on mobile.

## Mobile-first
- Touch controls are primary, keyboard is secondary.
- Portrait-friendly menus; gameplay targets landscape when the game benefits from it.
- Large hit targets and readable UI.
- Sprite atlases / compressed assets; avoid heavyweight real-time effects.

## Audio
Fast percussion, synth textures, short character stings and punchy impact sounds. Every action should feel responsive without requiring a huge soundtrack.
