# Mara Vale — Continuity Bible

Mara is the playable protagonist across all five games in The Long Run.

## Non-negotiables
- Mara is recognisable in every game.
- Red utility jacket and silver Courier 17 tag remain her visual anchors.
- Her messenger bag carries story-critical objects between chapters.
- Her personality is visible through short dialogue, not exposition dumps.
- Damage, scars, equipment and discoveries can accumulate across chapters.
- Each game ends by handing the player directly into the next chapter's premise.

## Visual language
- Red jacket is the strongest accent.
- Charcoal / black environment.
- Warm skin tones and brushed metal details.
- Silhouette must read at small scale.
- Avoid generic soldiers or anonymous player avatars.

## Story continuity
1. Rooftop Runner — data shard acquired.
2. One More Room — relay key acquired.
3. The Getaway — vehicle access and Voss pursuit established.
4. Dungeon Dash — blackout network discovered.
5. Street Heat — network key delivered; mystery resolved.

## Character progression
Mara never becomes a superhero. She becomes more capable because the player has survived the previous chapters. Her skills evolve from movement → combat → driving → traversal → mastery.
